Source Context Guide

Record the actual opened destination, the visible page identity, the selected specs, and the page context.

Context is what a fact depends on

A row’s meaning depends on where it came from and what the page showed at the time. Preserve that context, or the fact will lose its footing.

The opened destination

Record the URL you actually reached — not the label the row used. The destination is the current fact, not the pointer to it.

The visible page identity

Record the title or name shown on the page. A page that changed its title is not the same source it was before.

The selected specs

Note the color, size, and version as selected on the page. These are the conditions under which you read the listing.

The page context

Record the state of the page at the time you looked — what was visible, what was selectable, and what was not.

Field notePreserve where and when. A detail without its source is a rumor, not a record.

A worked record

  1. Destination: the page you opened, not the row link.
  2. Identity: the title the page shows.
  3. Specs: the color and size you selected.
  4. Context: what the page showed at that moment.
